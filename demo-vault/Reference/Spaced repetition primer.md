# Spaced repetition — primer

Short reference note. Not a summary of any one source.

## The mechanism

Recall is strengthened more by retrieving information than by re-reading it, and
the strengthening is largest when retrieval is *difficult but successful*. Spacing
reviews out increases difficulty in a controlled way.

## Practical shape

- Review just before you would have forgotten
- Intervals expand after each successful recall, contract after a failure
- The card must have exactly one answer; ambiguity reads as failure

## Where it breaks

Works well for atomic facts, badly for judgement. You cannot make a card for
"when should we split the onboarding flow". Attempting to usually produces a
card that tests the phrasing rather than the idea.

This is the limitation that matters for note-taking: most of what is worth
keeping in a working vault is not card-shaped.
