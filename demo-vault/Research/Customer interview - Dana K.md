# Customer interview — Dana K.

Independent strategy consultant, four active clients. Vault is ~1,400 notes,
organised by client folder. Has used Obsidian for two years, came from Notion.

## Capture

Records every client call. Transcribes about half of them, "the half I know I'll
need". The other half sits in an audio folder she describes as "a graveyard".

> The recording is free. The transcript is free now too. It's deciding which
> forty minutes matter that costs me.

## Organisation

Files manually, immediately after each call, and says this is the only reason
the vault is usable. Estimates 20-30 minutes a day on filing. Considers this
time wasted but necessary.

Tried an automated tagging plugin in 2024. Turned it off after a week — "it
tagged everything with #meeting and #notes, which is the same as tagging
nothing".

Bar for automation is therefore high: she will not trust suggestions she has to
check, because checking costs the same as filing.

## What she'd want

Action items pulled out of calls, with the timestamp, so she can jump back to
the moment in the audio. Everything else she'd rather do herself.

## Notes for follow-up

The "graveyard" folder is the clearest quantified pain we've heard. Ask for a
count next time.
