# Customer interview — Marco T.

PhD student, computational biology. Vault is ~300 notes, six months old, mostly
paper summaries and a running lab notebook.

## Capture

Reads on an iPad, highlights, syncs highlights in. Watches a lot of methods
walkthroughs on video and takes notes by hand in a paper notebook, then never
transcribes them.

Pasted a video link mid-call to show what he meant. Said if the link became a
structured note by itself he would "stop keeping the paper notebook tomorrow".

## Organisation

No folder structure to speak of. Everything is in the root. Uses search
exclusively, and says this works fine until about a year in, based on watching
labmates.

> I know the folders will matter eventually. I just can't make myself care yet.

Worth noting: this is the opposite of Dana. He has no filing habit to protect,
so automated organisation is upside with no trust cost.

## What he'd want

Paper → structured summary with methods and limitations separated out. Video →
same. He is uninterested in chat.

## Notes for follow-up

Two segments emerging: people protecting an existing system, and people who
never built one. They want almost opposite things from automation.
