# Open questions

Things that block a decision, roughly in order of how much they block.

## Blocking

**Does week-one churn correlate with vault size?**
Not currently measurable. Jo is scoping opt-in reporting. Everything in
[[Onboarding redesign overview]] rests on the answer.

**Where does the one-question branch live?**
If it's a modal it can be dismissed and we learn nothing. If it's a full screen
it's another step before value, which is the thing we're trying to remove.

## Non-blocking but unresolved

**Should first run offer sample content?**
Discussed and parked. The objection is that sample content in someone's own
vault feels like litter, and deleting it is the first thing the product asks
them to do.

**What happens to the setup screens we remove?**
They still need to exist somewhere. Probably settings, but nobody has looked at
whether the defaults are good enough to make them genuinely optional.

**Precision bar for suggestions**
From [[Interview themes]]: a suggestion that has to be checked is worth little
to the segment that already files manually. We have no number for what
precision would clear that bar, and asking people directly won't produce one.
